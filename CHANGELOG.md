## 2026-11-26: knowledge-base layout and licence

- The home page (English and Chinese) now opens with a short **Start here** section: what the repository is, how to use it, and links to the knowledge map. The methods catalogue below it is unchanged.
- New knowledge map in English and Chinese: [`docs/KNOWLEDGE_MAP.md`](docs/KNOWLEDGE_MAP.md) and [`docs/KNOWLEDGE_MAP.zh.md`](docs/KNOWLEDGE_MAP.zh.md). It covers the main concepts, tools by analysis step and a reference workflow, links each entry to its catalogue section, and lists the papers it cites.
- Four guide figures in [`docs/figures/guide/`](docs/figures/guide/README.md): two figures reproduced unchanged from Della Coletta et al. 2021 (Genome Biology, CC BY 4.0) and two schematics drawn by a script in the same folder.
- New [`LICENSE`](LICENSE) (MIT, for code) and [`NOTICE.md`](NOTICE.md) (CC BY 4.0 for text and figures made here; reproduced figures keep their own licence).
